The reference prices above were pulled from GoldRate24 at the time this page was built and will go stale — always click through to the source (or check goldpricez.com, isagha.com, or your local jeweler) for the live rate before calculating. Making charge (مصنعية) conventions vary: some jewelers charge a flat EGP amount per gram of the finished piece, others charge a percentage of the gold value, and some quote one flat fee for the whole item. Always confirm which method your jeweler uses. In reverse mode, the formula used is: Making Charge = (Final Price − Gold Value − Stone Charges) ÷ (1 + VAT%), and Making Charge per Gram = Making Charge ÷ Weight.
